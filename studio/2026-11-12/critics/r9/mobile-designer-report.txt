DIFFICULTY / ECONOMY READ (numbers)
- L1-13: 13/13 first-try wins, 10 of 13 at 3 stars. That is a gentle ramp, Royal Match style, correct. But the first losses only arrive at L14, 13 levels with no pressure; Block Blast/Royal Match put the first real "oh no" near L8-10. Stars drop from 3 to 1-2 from L11 on and almost never return to 3 (L16-L24 stars: 2,3,2,2,2,2,2,1,1): the 3-star bar from L18 up is a mean slower than a regular hand, so stars stop being a goal and become noise.
- L23 and L24 ended at 1 star with tLeft 5-6 s: these are near-losses, the right tension, and L20-L24 coin pace (+60 to +270 per level) is fine.
- Coins: 50 start, 2172 after L24 (about 90 per level). Cheapest skin 150, boosters 40-120, gild 600 (scr-shop.png). By L24 I could have bought most of the shop; with no pressure to spend, the sink is too shallow for the first hour. Boosters unlock at L6/L9/L13 for 40-120 coins, which is trivially cheap against income.
- Fail card: "RETRY +47 s" gift plus "+10 s keep cooking 3 left" is generous and clear. Good.

TOP 5 PROBLEMS
1. Stars stop meaning anything from L11 on (L16-L24: seven 2*, two 1*, one 3*). 3 stars on a normal-hand mean is nearly unreachable ("16s faster" on the L30 boss card). Change: make 3 stars reachable by a regular hand on about 1 level in 3, or tie the third star to something non-time (no chili burn, chain >= N) so there is a goal other than "faster".
2. Difficulty is flat then spiky: 13 straight first-try wins (L1-13), then 3 timeouts in the next 11 levels, each as a one-line "banana 0/4" or "bar 0.86" loss with nothing teaching the player what to change. Change: pull the first light pressure up to L8-10 (the brief's shorter first-try clock was there but invisible to me), and on a timeout show the single biggest missing line with an arrow hint.
3. Economy too rich: 2172 coins by L24 vs prices 40-1300. Nothing in the first hour makes me choose. Change: scale coin income down about 30 percent or add a mid-price sink (a second tier of boosters bought per level, a skin at 300 that is visible on the map) so the coin bar is a live goal.
4. Cold-start jump reliability / late-game length: L21 and L60 did not finish in 60 s of wall time under the bot (L60 85 s of game time and still going). If a normal hand really needs 85+ s for a level, that is long for a mobile level (mega-hits sit at 30-60 s). Check the late-level length or reduce item counts.
5. The unidentified 404 on every load (console). Find it, remove it.

THE ONE CHANGE THAT WOULD RAISE MY SCORE MOST: rework the star and pressure curve (problems 1 and 2): make 3 stars reachable on L11-L30 for a normal hand and move the first real loss pressure to L8-10. That makes every level a mini-goal instead of "win and move on". I would give 8.8 after it; with a coin-sink fix as well, 9.0.
