3 BEST THINGS
1. The feel. The pot, the confetti on bites, the x-chain counter, the orange glow on the rim, the crying pot on the fail card: it looks and reads like a premium hole game, and the Order Up / Boss Eaten stamp cards feel like rewards.
2. The loss design. A loss never feels like a wall: the fail card hands you extra seconds ("RETRY +65 s", "Extra time to help you! +23s") and a one-tap retry; on L8 that turned a loss into a win in about 25 s.
3. Meta that makes me want one more: skins with prices, "You can buy the Royal Pot!", boosters in the shop, cookbook slots, daily chest ladder, towns that change the look (picnic, market street, wooden pier) and a boss every 10 levels.

TOP 5 PROBLEMS
1. L1 to L7 are a walkover for a kid who plays Subway Surfers: 3 stars every time with 50+ s left on L2 to L4. Tension only starts around L6 to L7 and shows up properly at L11/L12 (5 to 8 s left). Change: move the first real "oh no" up to about L4 (a bigger ticket or a hot chili on L3 to L4), so a fast kid never sees a calm 3 stars at 60 s left twice in a row.
2. Cold boss levels were the ones I lost (L20, L41, L60 timed out on first try; samples only). Boss "open" moment is quiet: the locked padlock plus the pot just needing to be big enough, with nothing shouting "NOW" (S-L20 frames). Change: when the boss opens, flash the boss, shake the screen and play a stinger so a kid knows to rush it with the clock running.
3. First 10 levels give you a sloppy chain: my average hand's chain sat at 0 to 4 for most of L6 to L12, so the x-counter, the juiciest feedback, rarely got a showing. Change: widen the chain window a little in L1 to L12 or add a bite-streak burst (screen pulse) at x5 so it is felt even at low chains.
4. Fail card on a retry gift has no "why you lost" hint: it shows 0/3 and 0/4 but not what was in the way (I lost L8 and L41 with the pot circling). Change: highlight the line you were closest to and say "Eat [cheese] first, it was the slowest line", or point the first 5 s of the retry at the missing food.
5. Small polish: Feast end card puts the big "5" on the pot's belly (T-feast-card.png); the map card for "Daily" and "Feast" shows locked chips ("L6", "L12") that look tappable on desktop (D-map.png) and the desktop 404 for sibling-game thumbnails (above).

THE ONE CHANGE THAT WOULD RAISE MY SCORE MOST: make L3 to L10 spike earlier for a fast hand (tighter first-try clock plus a guaranteed hot-chili or boss-open moment on L4/L7), so a 13-year-old feels "that was close, one more" before L8. After it: 8.8/10.
