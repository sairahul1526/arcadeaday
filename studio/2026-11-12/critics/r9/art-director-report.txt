ART DIRECTOR REPORT, r9 (Slurp Pot, 57aa1b8 / frozen copy :8812). Screens and scripts in critics/r9/art-director/.

SCORE: 7.6/10 (art as shipped; the three-town ground/food problem is the thing holding it under 8.5)
Q1 Did the first 10 seconds hook me? YES. Title (p-title.png) is a premium 3D picnic with a glossy pot with eyes, big PLAY, tap-anywhere. L1 starts with a stuffed board, hand tip, glowing sandwich markers, and ends in ~10 s with ORDER UP (sheet-l1.png, L1a1-card.png). Reads like a real mobile game.
Q2 Did I hit retry or next without thinking? YES. Win card has a huge NEXT; fail card has a big RETRY +100 s and a sad pot (L5a1-card.png). L5 first try timed out (clock ~19 s on L5), retry won. Nothing made me stop.
Q3 Would I come back tomorrow? YES (art-wise): clay-diorama map, 10 pot skins, Daily/Feast chips, cookbook silhouettes give a collection pull. Not judged: real retention maths.

BLOCKERS: none confirmed. Console errors: none in any run (all arrays empty). Not tested: reload persistence, sound while paused, landscape 844x390 (not run; time and software-GL slowness), L22/L45 on desktop (script was still running at cut-off), real win/fail cards beyond L1 and L5. Caveat: headless uses tier "low", dpr 1.125, so board food edges look jagged/soft (L5a1-t000.png); I do not call that blurry rendering on a real device, but please check once on the Realme.
Driver note: a touch left held at level end blocked NEXT taps (my script, not the game). Win-card taps before ~1.5 s may be ignored; worth confirming that input lockout is short.

PLAYED: L1-L5 by real touch (CDP) on phone 390x844, average/normal: L1,2,3,4 won first try 3 stars in 10/11/14/22 sim-s; L5 lost once (timer), won on try 2. Sampled by __qaLevel on phone: L8, L21, L25, L30, L44, L50, L57, L60 (frames phone-L*-t*.png; my driver did not finish those cold levels, so no difficulty verdict). Desktop: title, map, daily, L3 only. Screens on phone: title, map, daily, feast, shop, cookbook, settings. Store covers in dist/portals/slurp-pot/media/ (cover-*, fb-*, icon, thumb): sheet-covers-a.png, sheet-covers-b.png.

3 BEST THINGS
1. Hero art: title, shop (pot skins), feast card, win/fail cards with the clay pot (crying pot on fail is great), clipboard ticket on the win card. Top of genre.
2. Store covers: swirl of soup with the cake falling in, huge eyes, readable at thumb size (thumb-200x120-title.jpg still reads). Strongest marketing asset.
3. Picnic town: warm, glossy, the gold guide trail with arrows (L3, L8) is very readable, glowing pot rim and x15 chain pop feel juicy.

TOP 5 PROBLEMS
1. Food vs ground in Market and Harbour is weak. L25 (phone-L25-t012.png): pretzels and chestnuts are brown on brown cobblestone, nearly invisible; the ticket's pineapple/broccoli/orange are nowhere on screen. L30 (phone-L30-t006.png): pretzels same issue. Harbour L50 and L60 (phone-L50-t000.png, phone-L60-t000.png): fries boxes, waffles, tacos, hot dogs are tan/orange on orange planks; only ice cream cones, blue gems and the pool read. Change: add a dark rim/outline or contact shadow to every food (2-3 px dark-brown outline pass), darken the Harbour plank value another 20-25 percent and desaturate it, and put a lighter or cooler mat under food clusters on cobble. Cobble to a cool grey-blue so warm foods pop.
2. Picnic gingham fights red/pink food. Desktop L3 (desk-L3-t000.png): tomatoes and strawberries on red check are fine on the cream mat, but lost on open cloth; L1/L2 cupcakes pink on pink. Change: lower the gingham saturation and contrast (pale pink/cream) on the play board, keep the saturated map version.
3. Ticket and edge-marker icons are small and food on later levels is sparse on screen. L57 (phone-L57-t012.png): 4-line ticket is cramped, 3 of 4 targets are only edge arrows, big empty planks. Change: bigger ticket icons (about 1.3x) and a count-down ring; seed a few target foods near the start position so the first screen always shows 2 targets.
4. UI overlaps on phone play. Booster stack (magnet/ice/ring + price) sits on the right edge and overlaps the food and trucks (phone-L25-t012.png, phone-L60-t000.png); the "Hold and drag" pill sits right on the pot at L44/L50/L60 even though it is not a first-time player's level (cold jump, so may be a QA artifact). Boss lock icon in the progress bar sits under the gold ticket glow at L50 and becomes unreadable. Change: booster stack to a smaller vertical rail at 60 percent size with price on tap only, lighten the gold ticket glow so the boss icon keeps contrast.
5. Map/menu polish. Desktop map (desk-screen-map.png): a narrow portrait panel with blurred side fills, wasted width; the Daily button's "+40" chip covers the word "Daily" and the L6/L12 badges overlap pill edges. Phone map (phone-screen-map.png): L7 node is clipped under the header pills and the two header pills ("Win 1 more for a chest" and "Daily day 2: Chest!") crowd the top. Shop: "Gild 600" is the loudest button for a player with 50 coins; booster icons are greyed so they look broken. Change: widen the desktop map art or tile a themed background, move "+40" to the top-right corner of the Daily icon, merge the two header pills into one rotating line, make Gild secondary until affordable.

THE ONE CHANGE THAT WOULD RAISE MY SCORE MOST: the food-vs-ground readability pass in Market and Harbour (outline or rim plus darker, cooler ground, issue 1). With that I would give 8.6.

Smaller notes: fail card item circles use flat emoji-style art unlike the 3D icons elsewhere (L5a1-card.png); lock chips on the Daily and Feast cards poke out of the card corner (phone-screen-daily.png), looks intentional but is borderline clipping; the first-try clock on L5 (about 19 s for a 3-item ticket) feels harsh to a first-time player, but my driver measured only one fail in five tries.
