READABILITY (phone 390x844, from a 68-year-old's view):
Good: ticket text and counts are large, buttons are huge (PLAY, NEXT, RETRY), cards are clean, the timer is a big red stopwatch, "Hold and drag" pulse text is clear, off-screen food arrows show the icon of what is needed. Tutorial-free first level is gentle.
Weak: (a) card small text such as "Next pot: Cauldron, 32 more coins", "100 s extra this try", "Win 3 more for a chest" and the Cookbook star rows is only about 11-13 px of 390 and low contrast gold on cream; (b) the clock icon + "0:28/0:40" on the win card is unlabeled and I could not tell whether it is time used or time left; (c) the "3 stars: 0:02" chip on L41 (d-L41-f150.png) reads as a countdown to something bad, not "3-star window".

TOP 5 PROBLEMS
1. Console 404 /site.css on every load (index.html line 20). Remove the stylesheet link or serve the file; zero-error console is a ship gate item.
2. Near-loss and 1-star cluster for a slow hand at L9 (7.4 s left, 1 star) and L12 (5.0 s left, 1 star): the first-try shorter clock stacks with a slow hand so the end of the first 12 levels feels like barely scraping through. I want a slow-hand win to leave about 15 s spare on L9-L12, or the "Extra time to help you" nudge to fire earlier than it did.
3. Win card time readout (a-card-win-L6-5.png, b-card-win-L7-6.png): the stopwatch "0:28" has no label. Say "Time left 0:28" or "Time 1:10". Same card: the green "Daily Order" button, the "You can buy the Rice Cooker!" banner and gift/magnet chips are stacked tight above NEXT (L6 card); NEXT is lower in importance in the layout than it should be, and a grandparent may hit the green button first. Give NEXT the top spot and show the Daily offer once as a small chip.
4. Small low-contrast text on cards and map banners (see readability list). Raise the 11-13 px gold-on-cream lines ("Next pot: ...", "N s extra this try", "Win 3 more for a chest") to at least 15 px with darker ink.
5. Phone map has an empty brown band under the Daily/Feast/Cookbook/Shop buttons (s-phone-map.png, about 130 px wasted) while the level path is cut off at the top; push the buttons to the bottom edge or give the path the space. And the "3 stars: 0:02" chip on late levels (d-L41-f150.png) should hide once 3 stars is out of reach instead of showing a 2-second countdown.
