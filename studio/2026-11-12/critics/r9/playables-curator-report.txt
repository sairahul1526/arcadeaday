TOP 5 PROBLEMS
1. Banner overlap at boss unlock + size-up (L25, L41, probably every level where both coincide). Change: queue banners, or let "Bigger pot!" wait 1.2 s until BOSS UNLOCKED! has left, or put them on different rows.
2. First-try clock swing. L5 (Gate Crasher): first try is a 30 s clock (3 stars/45 s-ish levels before it had 60-99 s), the average-hand driver lost at 78 %, and the card then offers "RETRY +100 s" (130 s total) (p1-31 .. p1-38). L21 first try 40 s, lost, "+54 s"; L45 41 s, lost "SO CLOSE", "+74 s". A 4x swing between try 1 and try 2 feels like a rubber band to a player and removes the stakes of try 2 (it never fails). Change: cap the gift at about +30-40 s, and keep the first-try clock at no less than ~60 % of the level's natural time so a new player is not told "Out of time!" on L5 as the first fail of their life.
3. Win card overlaps its own art: the red "Chain x26!" / "Chain x16!" ribbon cuts across the clipboard and the pot picture (D1-02-win-L1.png, YT-wincard.png). Change: move the ribbon below the art, or inline it next to "You ate N!". Not text-on-text, but it reads as a layering bug.
4. Desktop 1440x900 play camera is very pulled back: the pot is ~100 px of 1440 on L1 (D1-01-play-L1.png), the fence and trees dominate and the first-10-seconds feel is weaker than on a phone. Change: tighten the desktop camera about 25-30 % on L1-L5, pull back only as the pot grows.
5. Landscape 844x390: the map is a small serpentine in the middle with blurred side bars (LS-map.png) and L3 play shows a thin ticket at the top-left (LS-L3-land.png). Playable and no clipping, but the map is cramped and the title bar buttons ("how to play", "more games") sit tight. Change: widen the landscape map to use the side space, or let the serpentine scale to the full width.

THE ONE CHANGE THAT WOULD RAISE MY SCORE MOST: fix the stacked banners (problem 1) and moderate the first-try clock / +100 s retry swing (problem 2). With both fixed, plus the ribbon moved off the win art, I would give 8.9-9.0.
