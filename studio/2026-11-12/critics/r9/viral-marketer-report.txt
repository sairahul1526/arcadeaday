CLIP VERDICT (promise vs delivery)
The clip is a strong thumb-stopper: opening on 20+ eaten with a x9 chain and a countdown, juice boxes exploding into a pile, parasols and the giant cake as the payoff, ORDER UP stamp, end card with the pot and cake. The caption hook "start the size of a pea... eat the whole park... even the giant cake" is the right story, and the game does deliver the cake as the L20 boss bite (s20.jpg, b-L20-t063-0.png). But the clip overpromises scale on first contact: it shows chain x45 and 136 eaten, while L1-L5 gave me chain x9-x19 and 76-108 eaten on small tables of cupcakes. The cake appears at L20, not in the first minute. The clip never shows a fail, the timer pressure, the chili, or the boss UI.

3 BEST THINGS
1. Visual identity: warm toy-town, glossy gold pot with eyes, strong against the usual hole-game flat grey. The cover (cover-800x1200-title.jpg) alone would stop a scroll: cake lifting out of the pot, big gold title.
2. The growth/swallow loop reads instantly: ticket icons, food that glows when edible, "Too big!" tip, dotted guide trail, and a satisfying bite payoff on bosses (basket L10, cake L20, lighthouse L60).
3. The cards: one big button, stars, "You ate N!", chain ribbon, coin counter. Win and fail both push the next try with no menu (a-L1-win-0.png, b-L5-fail-0.png).

TOP 5 PROBLEMS
1. Clip opening starts after the growth story. Frame 1 (clip/f01.jpg) is "24 eaten" with the pot already mid-size, yet the on-screen hook says "pea-sized pot". Change: first 0.8 s should show the pea pot beside one cupcake, then smash-cut to the fed pot; or reword the hook to "Eat everything in the park" so text and picture agree.
2. Clip dead stretch around 10-13 s (f10-f13): pot on an almost empty lawn with tiny scattered food while the counter climbs 123 to 136. The energy drops just before the cake. Change: cut these 3 s, or put a bonus cluster in front of the pot so the last bites stay dense.
3. Title screen has zero promise (cold-title.png): logo plus PLAY. A scrolling-from-social player gets no "why". Change: add a one-line tagline under the logo ("Start tiny. Eat the whole park.") and show the cake on the title scene.
4. Day-one return hook is thin: Daily and Feast are greyed with "Win 6 / 12 more levels" (scr-ph-daily.png, scr-ph-feast.png), and the cookbook is all "?" silhouettes. Change: let the Daily be playable from L1 (easy mode) or show the NEXT skin teaser with a coin bar prominently after the first win, and name tomorrow's reward on the L3-L5 win cards.
5. Fail card is flat (b-L5-fail-0.png, c-L30-fail-0.png): same sad pot every time, no near-miss line. The cold L30 fail on "average-normal" and the L5 first-try fail came with no "so close, 3 more bites" hook. Change: show the remaining ticket count or a "N s short" line and a shaking pot with the missing food highlighted, to make retry feel like the next try wins.
Also minor: desktop map (s_dt.jpg) is a narrow column between blurred sides; the boss win cards on L10/L60 say "Finish 47s faster for 3 stars" when 0:45 was left, which reads confusing.

THE ONE CHANGE THAT WOULD RAISE MY SCORE MOST
Re-cut the clip's first second and hook line so the picture and words tell the same "pea to park" growth story (pea pot beside one cupcake, then the 24-eaten chain), and drop the empty-lawn stretch. That is the piece that decides whether a stranger taps; the game itself already delivers. After it I would give 8.9.
